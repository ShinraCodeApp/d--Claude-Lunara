## English (en-US)

**Title (30):**
Lunara: Period Tracker & Luna

**Short description (80):**
Track your cycle, symptoms and mood. Predictions, reminders and Luna, your AI 🌙

**Full description:**

Lunara helps you understand your menstrual cycle and wellbeing, month after month. 🌙

🗓️ CYCLE TRACKING
• Log your period, flow and duration in one tap
• Estimates of your next period and fertile window
• Calendar with your phases: menstrual, follicular, ovulatory and luteal
• Works offline: your logs are saved and synced when you're back online

📊 SYMPTOMS & WELLBEING
• Cramps, headaches, bloating, acne, mood, energy and sleep
• Charts and patterns to see how you feel in each phase
• PDF report to bring to your doctor or gynecologist

💬 LUNA, YOUR ASSISTANT
• Ask Luna about your cycle, PMS or ovulation
• Answers tailored to your current phase
• General, educational information: Luna does not diagnose or prescribe medication

🔔 REMINDERS
• Heads-up before your period and when your estimated fertile window starts
• Alert if your period is late
• Pill, water and daily log reminders

🌱 YOUR LUNAR GARDEN
• Earn points and achievements for taking care of yourself
• Streaks and missions that make tracking fun

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
• Your health data is yours: Lunara never sells it
• PIN lock and discreet mode
• Export or delete your data anytime

💎 PLANS
• Free: cycle, symptoms, calendar and basic Luna
• Premium: advanced statistics, extended predictions and exclusive features

For users 18+.
